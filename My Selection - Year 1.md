## The fix

Semester 2 offers **6 optionals** (CS242, CS402, CS417, CS425, CS426, CS427) plus compulsory
CS290. Take **5 of the 6** and the split lands exactly on **30 / 30**:

- Semester 1 = CS310 + 5 optionals = 30 credits
- Semester 2 = CS290 + 5 optionals = 30 credits

You currently hold 3 of the Semester 2 optionals, so: **add 2 from Semester 2, drop 2 from Semester 1.**

### Two Semester-1 modules are locked in

- **CS320** — [[CS402 - Parallel & Distributed Systems\|CS402]] lists *"CS320 or equivalent course on Computer Networks"* as a pre-requisite.
- **CS422** — [[CS427 - Autonomous Mobile Robotics\|CS427]] lists *"CS422 Robotics & Automation or equivalent"*.

Dropping either kills a Semester 2 module you've already chosen. Safe to drop:
**CS357, CS401, CS404, CS416, CS424** — nothing depends on them.
