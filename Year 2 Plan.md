# Year 2 — CSAF62 (60 credits)

> [!note] Year 2 pass rules
> **50%** per module; compensation permitted **only for module marks over 40%**.
> Four of the five compulsory modules are also **required** — no compensation at all.
> Resits, where permitted, are **capped at 50%**.

> [!warning] The 30% continuous-assessment hurdle
> [[CS613 - Advanced Concepts in Object-Oriented Programming\|CS613]],
> [[CS607 - Requirements Engineering & System Design\|CS607]] and
> [[CS616 - Practical Cryptography\|CS616]] each state:
> *"A mark of at least 30% must be attained in continuous assessment. Otherwise the module
> mark of the student may be capped at 35%."*
> A capped 35% is below the Year 2 pass mark **and** below the 40% compensation floor —
> so dropping CA in CS613 costs you a required module.

> [!important] The credit arithmetic leaves one free choice
> Compulsory total = 7.5 + 10 + 5 + 5 + 25 = **52.5**. 60 − 52.5 = **7.5**, and every
> optional is worth exactly 7.5 — so you pick **exactly one** of CS607 / CS616 / CS621B,
> and it sits in Semester 1. Semester 1 = 30 credits, Semester 2 = 30 credits.
