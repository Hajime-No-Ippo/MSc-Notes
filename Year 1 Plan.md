# Year 1 — CSAF6 (60 credits)

All Year 1 modules are worth **5 credits**. Two are compulsory (10 credits), so
**10 optional modules** must be chosen to reach 60.

> [!note] Year 1 pass rules
> **40%** per module. Compensation permitted as per undergraduate level of **35%**.
> Every Year 1 module states *"Could the result of this module be capped? **No**"*,
> resits are permitted in all 20, and no Year 1 module carries a minimum-CA hurdle.

> [!tip] Assessment shape
> Written exams are **120 minutes** everywhere they exist.
> **100% CA, no exam:** [[CS385 - Mobile Application Development\|CS385]], [[CS242 - User-Centred Software Engineering\|CS242]]
> **100% exam, no CA:** [[CS356 - Image and Optical Processing\|CS356]], [[CS404 - Artificial Intelligence & Language Processing\|CS404]], [[CS416 - Cryptography\|CS416]], [[CS430 - Advanced Concepts & Issues in Comp.Sci. 1\|CS430]], [[CS417 - Numerical Computation\|CS417]]
