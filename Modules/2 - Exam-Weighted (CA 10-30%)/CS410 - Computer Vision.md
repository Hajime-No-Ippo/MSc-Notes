## 📖 Overview

In this course students will be given a broad introduction to the field of computer vision, including the following topics: Introduction to computer vision and its applications; cameras and sensing; visual attributes, histograms and kernel density estimation; contour extraction; interest point detection and matching; machine learning and deep neural networks for computer vision.

## 🎯 Learning Outcomes

On successful completion of the module, students should be able to:

- Mathematically model the image formation process, and calibrate a given camera using this model.
- Describe the different types of image sensors and identify a suitable camera setup based on the requirements of a computer vision system.
- Explain how histograms and kernel density estimation techniques can be used to solve a range of problems in computer vision.
- Implement the 2D convolution and correlation operations and describe the mathematical properties of these operations.
- Describe, compare, and implement approaches to image contour extraction.
- Explain and contrast approaches to interest point extraction, description, and matching.
- Understand the fundamentals of machine learning and deep neural networks and their application in the field of computer vision.
- Understanding the ethical and societal issues associated with computer vision and machine learning.
