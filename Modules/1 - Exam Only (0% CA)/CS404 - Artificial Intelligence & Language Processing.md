## 📖 Overview

Introduction to both artificial intelligence (AI) and natural language processing (NLP). Defining intelligence and multiple Intelligences, Turing's imitation game; ethics. problem-solving, state-spaces, generate and test, game playing; two-player games, graph-searching; optimisation strategies; biologically inspired computation. Introduction to neural networks and deep learning, text processing, tagging, parsing language (especially English), semantics and knowledge representation; embeddings, knowledge graphs, information retrieval, overview of reinforcement learning. Large language models (GPT-4), transformer models, prompt, temperature, hallucinations, and bias.

## 🎯 Learning Outcomes

On successful completion of the module, students should be able to:

- Explain the underlying principles, advantages and limitations of the AI approaches to problem solving.
- Demonstrate the application of artificial intelligence techniques to problem solving.
- Describe AI algorithms with their application to a range of problem types.
- Assess the strengths and weaknesses of AI approaches to different problem types.
- Compare classical and deep learning (embedding) approaches to text and language.
- Assess deep learning and symbolic approaches to language understanding.
