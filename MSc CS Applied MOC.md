## ⚖️ Marks & Standards (exception to the general regs)

| | Year 1 | Year 2 |
|---|---|---|
| **Pass mark per module** | 40% | 50% |
| **Compensation** | permitted as per undergraduate level, **35%** | permitted **only for module marks over 40%** |

> [!warning] Required modules
> A **required module** must be passed **without compensation** to progress in the subject.
> In MSCF2 these are all in Year 2: [[CS613 - Advanced Concepts in Object-Oriented Programming|CS613]],
> [[CS642 - MSc Computer Science (Applied) Project|CS642]],
> [[CS662 - Work Placement Documentation (MSc Applied)|CS662]],
> [[CS663 - Industrial Work Placement (MSc Applied)|CS663]].

> [!danger] The 30% CA hurdle — three Year 2 modules
> [[CS613 - Advanced Concepts in Object-Oriented Programming|CS613]],
> [[CS607 - Requirements Engineering & System Design|CS607]] and
> [[CS616 - Practical Cryptography|CS616]] each require **at least 30% in continuous
> assessment**, or the module mark *may be capped at 35%* — below the Year 2 pass mark
> and below the 40% compensation floor. CS613 is also a required module, so the CA there
> is load-bearing twice over.

> [!question] One inconsistency to check with the department
> [[CS642 - MSc Computer Science (Applied) Project|CS642]] is a Year 2 module, but its
> descriptor states a **pass standard of 40%** while the course-structure page sets the
> Year 2 standard at 50%. Both are recorded verbatim in the notes.
