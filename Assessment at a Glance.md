## Patterns worth knowing

- **No module in the programme can be capped** — every descriptor answers *"Could the result of this module be capped?"* with **No**.
- **Exam length is uniform per year:** 120 minutes in Year 1, 180 minutes in Year 2 — except [[CS670 - Computation & Complexity (MSc Applied)\|CS670]], which keeps the 120-minute format.
- **Pass standard tracks the year, with one anomaly:** Year 1 modules pass at 40%, Year 2 at 50% — but [[CS642 - MSc Computer Science (Applied) Project\|CS642]] states **40%** on its descriptor despite being a Year 2 module. The course-structure page's Year 2 rule says 50%. Worth confirming with the department.
- **Three modules have a minimum-CA hurdle** ([[CS607 - Requirements Engineering & System Design\|CS607]], [[CS613 - Advanced Concepts in Object-Oriented Programming\|CS613]], [[CS616 - Practical Cryptography\|CS616]]): under 30% in CA and the module mark may be capped at 35%.
- **Two modules allow no resit at all:** [[CS642 - MSc Computer Science (Applied) Project\|CS642]] and [[CS621B - Databases\|CS621B]]. [[CS662 - Work Placement Documentation (MSc Applied)\|CS662]] and [[CS663 - Industrial Work Placement (MSc Applied)\|CS663]] state nothing about resits.
- **Year 2 resits are capped at 50%** where they are permitted.
- **Exam-free modules (100% CA):** [[CS385 - Mobile Application Development\|CS385]], [[CS242 - User-Centred Software Engineering\|CS242]], [[CS642 - MSc Computer Science (Applied) Project\|CS642]], [[CS621B - Databases\|CS621B]], [[CS662 - Work Placement Documentation (MSc Applied)\|CS662]], [[CS663 - Industrial Work Placement (MSc Applied)\|CS663]].
- **CA-free modules (100% exam):** [[CS356 - Image and Optical Processing\|CS356]], [[CS404 - Artificial Intelligence & Language Processing\|CS404]], [[CS416 - Cryptography\|CS416]], [[CS430 - Advanced Concepts & Issues in Comp.Sci. 1\|CS430]], [[CS417 - Numerical Computation\|CS417]], [[CS616 - Practical Cryptography\|CS616]].
